Rules we run by

Six rules, mostly from Abdullah, that fit Sketchie

Know the what before the tool

Outreach starts manual and public. Apollo, Clay or scraped lists come after we know the message works.

Spend goes through Manuel, nothing else does

Zero-spend first batches. Then one budget ask, with the numbers from batch 1.

Outreach is the core, content is a branch

Lessons and pages compound but take months. They never become the whole plan.

Full gear on what we're sure of

Sure: public replies and the launch posts. Test small: library and SEO.

The board tells the story

Every play is an Asana ticket with an owner and a daily log. Nobody has to ask what someone did.

Don't pull volume we can't support

Scale a play only while the daily failure log stays manageable for Andi and Alex.
