The science

The open standard for explainer-video quality.

The published research on how people learn from video, turned into numeric rules the generator has to obey. The whole spec, grades and all.

Read the spec The full citations

Every rule wears its evidence grade.

Anyone can claim to be built on science. Grading each claim is what lets you tell a verified effect from a working assumption.

Verified

Replicated in published research, with an effect size worth acting on.

Product

2 rules

The numbers the pipeline must hit.

The six that shape every scene. The full contract is below.

12-30sScene length

One idea per scene.

3-7Elements

Counted at the end of the scene.

2-5sReveal spacing

Never two closer than 1.2 seconds.

500msWord sync

Each element lands on its word.

140-160Words per minute

Spoken register, not read.

3 wordsLabel limit

A keyword, never a sentence.

Question

Every video closes by asking the viewer to produce the answer.

Pause

The video holds, so the answer is theirs before it is given.

One line recap

Then the loop closes. The question is never replaced by a summary.

The whole contract.

Eighteen parameters the generator is checked against on every render, each carrying the grade of the evidence behind it.
