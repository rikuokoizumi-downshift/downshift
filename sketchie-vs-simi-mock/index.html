Before this ships

The page now live at sketchie.ai/vs-simi/ is dated July 2026. Eight of its claims no longer match what Lamina Labs publishes. This mock is built on today's figures; the live page still carries these.

  1. Top tier. The live page says “Published tiers up to $499.99/mo” and “custom branding $499.99/mo”, but their highest published self-serve plan is Max at $99.99, and branding now sits in Business with no published price.
  2. Speed. The live page says “under 20 seconds”, but their site now says about 40 seconds for a one-minute explainer.
  3. Length. The live page says “up to 1 hour in one shot”, and the whole “About that 1-hour video” section, but their FAQ now says most plans go up to about five minutes, longer only on Business.
  4. Length steps. The live page says “preset lengths, 1-minute steps”, but their credits are priced per second, so the one-minute-steps contrast no longer holds.
  5. Funding. The live page says “they raised $3.5M”, but reporting puts it at $4.8M, and their site lists three backers.
  6. API. The live page says “3x their published app rate, gated to top tiers”, but no per-tier API pricing is published on their site today.
  7. Free tier. The live page says July intel said 120 free credits on signup, but no free tier is published on their site today.
  8. Our own line. The live page says “We launched this week” on a page dated July 2026, but launch week was 26 October to 1 November.

An honest comparison

Sketchie vs Simi

We wrote this, so read it with that in mind. Every number below comes from a page one of us publishes, and it is dated. Where Simi is better, this page says so, and it says so first.

Checked 31 October 2026. Both products change their pricing.

Credit where it is due

Simi is a serious product from a serious team. They generate a one-minute whiteboard explainer in about forty seconds, they bet on pedagogical pacing while everyone else chased cinematic AI video, and they made enough noise about this category that the rest of us are here.

If raw first-generation speed is what you care about most, they are better at it than we are. We built Sketchie around what happens after that first generation.

What that leaves out

  • Our rate uses Icons, our cheapest style. Doodles and Icon KIT cost more.
  • A rate per minute counts first renders. It does not count the second attempt after a line comes out wrong, which on a one-shot tool is another full generation.

Where each of us is ahead

Their column first, because a comparison page that opens with its own advantages is an advert.

Simi is ahead on

  • Price per minute. At list price they are cheaper. Their Max plan works out near 13 cents a finished minute, our Pro near 38 cents in our cheapest style.
  • First generation. About 40 seconds for a one-minute explainer, by their own figure. We take a few minutes.
  • Languages. 80+ against our 79, and theirs covers source documents as well as narration.
  • Long videos. Past six minutes we turn material into a chaptered series. Their Business plan takes longer runtimes in one piece.
  • Document formats. They name PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Markdown and plain text. We say document input without listing formats.
  • Track record. Y Combinator, Foundation Capital and Link Ventures are on their page. We launched in October.

Sketchie is ahead on

  • Starting at $9.99. Their lowest published plan is $19.99. If you make a handful of videos a month, the cheaper plan wins on total spend even at a higher rate per minute.
  • You can try it without paying. Two free videos, no card. We could not find a free tier published on their site.
  • A revision is not a new video. Editing and previewing are free and each video includes a re-render. On a tool with no partial regeneration, every fix is a fresh generation at full price.
  • Commercial use is not gated. Their own FAQ puts commercial rights behind a Business agreement. Our Terms give you ownership of the videos on any plan.
  • Branding has a published price. $49.99 on Pro, against a Business plan you have to ask about.
  • You see the price first. The exact Credit quote appears before anything starts, is held while the video is made, and is only charged on delivery.
